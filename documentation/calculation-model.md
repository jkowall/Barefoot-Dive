# Calculation model

## Implemented model

The pure engine uses 16-compartment ZH-L16C inert-gas kinetics for nitrogen and helium, with coefficient metadata identifying the DecoTengu/OSTC-derived set (`decotengu-0.14.1-coefficients-v1`). It tracks tissue state through explicit exposure events, computes GF-adjusted ceilings, rounds stops to the configured grid, and schedules deterministic ascent/stops. Inputs and outputs use branded metres, seconds, bar absolute, litres, and fractions.

Breathing events support OC bottom/travel/deco gases, CCR diluent/setpoint exposure, CCR setpoint changes, and bailout. The CCR bailout plan starts from the exact tissue/depth/runtime state at `bailoutTriggerSecondsAtDepth`. Gas accounting integrates ambient pressure over each segment and applies fixed, custom, thirds, sixths, or rock-bottom reserves, reporting insufficiency and reserve-crossing diagnostics.

### CCR setpoints (engine 0.2.0)

With `lowSetpointBar`, the loop is closed at the low setpoint from the surface, switches up to `setpointBar` at `setpointActivationDepthM`, and switches down when leaving `setpointDeactivationDepthM` on ascent (absent means the switch-up depth). The rule is "leave the depth": the high setpoint is held at every depth at or below the switch-down depth, including a stop there, and the switch happens immediately before the next ascent leg. A switch-down depth that falls between stops or shallower than the last stop splits that leg without adding a stop, in the no-stop path, the first-stop search, the stop loop, and the final leg. In low-setpoint mode a setpoint change is exposed on the lower of the two setpoints for the convention's switch duration (0 s, or 5 s under the Shearwater preset); legacy inputs keep the 0.1.0 exposure on the new setpoint. Loop inert pressure is `ambient − water vapor − setpoint`, split by the diluent's inert ratio; where the setpoint exceeds that, the loop is modeled as oxygen at ambient pressure. That clamp would credit off-gassing the loop cannot deliver, so the open-water planner never holds the high setpoint shallower than its achievable depth, `(setpoint + water vapor − surface pressure) × metres per bar` (3.6 m for 1.3 bar at sea level). A shallower switch-down depth, including 0, is deepened to that depth with a `CCR_SWITCH_DOWN_DEEPENED` warning; this also keeps the clamp out of the no-stop decision. Explicit event plans apply the same floor to the setpoint actually held when the ascent starts. Because the low setpoint can load inert gas on the rest of a leg that crosses the switch-down depth, low-setpoint mode re-checks the arrival ceiling after each such leg: the first stop moves one grid step deeper, or the current stop is held on the high setpoint, until the arrival clears. The low setpoint must be 0.5–1.6 bar, not above the high setpoint or the bottom PPO₂ limit, and achievable at the surface. A diluent hypoxic at the surface is accepted on the loop with a flush warning; a diluent whose own PPO₂ exceeds a setpoint is flagged because the model then carries more inert gas and less oxygen than the loop can hold.

Without `lowSetpointBar` the legacy convention applies: open-circuit diluent from the surface to the activation depth and again after arriving at it on ascent, with the diluent required to be breathable over that range. Legacy CCR breathing and non-hypoxic OC schedules are unchanged from engine 0.1.0. `src/engine/legacy-golden.test.ts` guards seven fixture inputs against digests captured from 0.1.0; it is a regression guard, not a proof over all inputs. Inputs without the new fields can still gain `CCR_DILUENT_PPO2_ABOVE_SETPOINT` and `CCR_BAILOUT_TRIGGER_PPO2_HIGH` warnings, can be rejected by `CCR_BAILOUT_SURFACE_REQUIRED`, `CCR_BAILOUT_COVERAGE_GAP`, or `OC_GAS_HYPOXIC`, can be rerouted by the hypoxic waypoint or the stop move below, and are rejected when their bailout plan cannot finish decompression.

### Bailout, dil-out, and hypoxic legs

The effective bailout set is `bailoutGases`, plus the diluent when `diluentBailout` is true. Gas choice is richest oxygen, then least helium; for an identical mix a dedicated bailout is preferred over the diluent. Validation requires at least one bailout gas that is switch-eligible (breathable and no deeper than its switch depth) at the target depth and at the surface, and the union of every gas's eligible depth interval must cover the depths between (`CCR_BAILOUT_COVERAGE_GAP` names the first gap). A trigger gas above the bottom PPO₂ limit is flagged but not reselected. For dil-out the diluent needs its own cylinder and a diver-entered `diluentPreBailoutUseL`; the bailout ledger starts that cylinder at its full volume minus the entered use and any modeled open-circuit diluent breathing before the trigger, and keeps the reserve policy on the full volume. Loop make-up, ADV, flush, wing, and suit use are not modeled.

An open-circuit ascent leg whose end would put the breathed gas below the minimum PPO₂ is split at the deepest depth, no shallower than that gas's hypoxic floor, where a preferred gas is switch-eligible, and breathing is updated there. Legs that stay breathable are committed exactly as before. Every plan (primary, bailout, and cave event plans) is then scanned, and any open-circuit segment below the minimum PPO₂ at either end is a fatal `OC_GAS_HYPOXIC`. One tolerance (1e-8 bar) is shared by the scheduler, validation, and this guard.

A stop can fail to clear on its gas, for example a lean trimix at the last stop when the richer gas is eligible only shallower. When a schedule would reach the 48-hour limit, the ascent is recomputed once. At the stuck stop depth or shallower, a stop that cannot clear may move to the deepest shallower depth where a preferred open-circuit gas becomes switch-eligible, if that depth is inside the ceiling; deeper stops are scheduled exactly as in the first run. Decompression continues there, which can be shallower than the configured last stop, and the plan carries `OC_STOP_MOVED_FOR_GAS_SWITCH`. Schedules that already clear are never recomputed. A CCR plan whose bailout plan still reaches the 48-hour or iteration limit is rejected, with the failure attributed to the bailout plan.

### Gas-only planning

`gasOnly` plans (open water only) carry gases without cylinders; a per-gas `maximumPPO2` replaces the cylinder PPO₂ ceiling. The ledger reports used volume, the reserve, and `requiredVolumeL`, the minimum surface volume to carry, by inverting the cylinder rules: thirds keeps a third of the start, so the start is 1.5 × used; sixths keeps two thirds, so 3 × used; a custom reserve is added; rock bottom uses the same formula over that gas's ascent, deco, exit, and bailout segments. A fixed minimum-pressure reserve needs a cylinder size and is rejected. A gas whose reserve comes out as zero, such as a travel gas under rock bottom (which counts only ascent, deco, exit, and bailout use), is flagged `GAS_ONLY_RESERVE_ZERO`. A per-gas `maximumPPO2` is accepted only in gas-only plans (`GAS_MAXIMUM_PPO2_REQUIRES_GAS_ONLY`); cylinder plans take the limit from the cylinder. Capacity, pressures, unusable residual gas, per-cylinder minimums, and reserve crossings are not checked, and such entries are never reported as sufficient. Dil-out and cave plans require cylinders.

### Cave

The cave module supplies explicit penetration and exit events and scenario transformations to the same production event engine. Each route segment resolves a physically accessible, depth-eligible gas/cylinder; stage drops and recovery determine later accessibility. Gas-derived maximum time/distance uses a bounded search over proportionally scaled copies of the entered route. Every candidate is rerun through the tissue engine, ascent/decompression scheduler, and every actually used cylinder ledger; CCR candidates use the exact end-of-penetration bailout plan. A numeric maximum is emitted only after a safe lower bound and unsafe upper bound are both established. Otherwise the result carries `GAS_LIMIT_UNDETERMINED` and omits the number. The reported turn pressure and minimum-required pressure identify the same actual cylinder, while the result separately identifies the cylinder with the limiting reserve margin.

Thirds and sixths expose their operational turn thresholds separately from the minimum pressure required at the planned turn. The CCR cave-level ledger is the bailout contingency ledger; normal CCR oxygen metabolism and diluent/loop consumables remain explicitly unmodeled. The module does not invent a ceiling-safe overhead route. A cave route must explicitly model the route and be checked; cave behavior is experimental pending qualified cave-diver review. In low-setpoint CCR mode, descent and penetration legs switch up at the activation depth, exit legs switch down at the switch-down depth, and the final ascent stays on the loop, so no open-circuit surface gas is required for it. The switch-down depth must be at or below the depth where the high setpoint is achievable (`CAVE_CCR_SWITCH_DOWN_TOO_SHALLOW`), because route legs are explicit. CCR turn limits come from the bailout ledger alone and do not subtract loop breathing on the diluent cylinder. With dil-out, cave gas choice uses the planner's shared ordering (richest oxygen, then least helium, then a dedicated bailout before the diluent); other cave plans keep the 0.1.0 ordering. The dil-out diluent cylinder's operational turn pressure is never below the pressure its exit and reserve require, because its gauge falls during penetration and its exit is on open circuit at bailout RMV. The penetration-limit search scales the entered pre-bailout diluent use with each candidate route, never below the entered value.

## Presets and limitations

`barefoot-zhl16c-v1`, `multideco-zhlc-compatible-v1`, and `shearwater-petrel3-v103-compatible-v1` are scheduling/settings presets with explicit experimental status. Their labels do not claim MultiDeco, Shearwater, firmware, or schedule parity. The implementation does not include repetitive dives, planner CNS or OTU accumulation, IBCD validation, or arbitrary profile authoring as validated capabilities. Low-setpoint modeling changes PPO₂ exposure, but the planner reports no CNS or OTU total; CNS is available only in Tools. The first-stop search does not re-check the arrival ceiling on open-circuit and legacy CCR legs, unchanged from 0.1.0. With helium-heavy gases, slow compartments can load during the ascent so the first stop sits above the GF-low ceiling; for example, a 60 m Tx12/60 bailout stops first at 27 m under a 29.1 m ceiling. Only low-setpoint legs are re-checked.

The 11 calculator functions are production code paths, but their existence and unit tests do not establish operational safety. CNS uses the documented NOAA single-exposure table only from PPO2 0.6 through 1.6 bar. Barefoot Dive linearly interpolates between the listed rows as an explicit versioned implementation policy; NOAA is not cited as defining that interpolation. Values outside the table are rejected instead of extrapolated. CCR oxygen metabolism and diluent/loop consumables are not inferred from OC RMV in the normal CCR ledger.

The Tools redesign adds interaction policy around these functions, not a new decompression model. Best Mix and END use versioned PPO2/narcotic-policy identifiers and require the actual oxygen and helium fractions. With oxygen and nitrogen treated as narcotic, `END = ((Pambient × (1 − FHe)) − Psurface) × metersPerBar`; with nitrogen alone narcotic, `END = ((Pambient × FN2 ÷ 0.79) − Psurface) × metersPerBar`. Best Mix solves those equations in reverse while respecting the PPO2 ceiling and returns the complete active-constraint set.

SAC / RMV accepts either direct measured surface-gas volume or cylinder water volume plus a gauge-pressure drop, preserving the selected mode and source values. A moving-depth sample uses the arithmetic mean of start/end absolute pressure and therefore assumes depth changes linearly with time. Emergency Gas (Rock Bottom / Minimum Gas and Simplified Bailout) evaluates only an explicitly entered schedule through the same gas-integration primitive used by planner ledgers. OC requires an explicit team size and can return schedule gas without a cylinder or add one-cylinder pressure/sufficiency output; CCR requires one bailout cylinder. Every cylinder check requires an explicit reserve, including an intentional zero. It does not generate a decompression schedule or a CCR bailout plan; CCR bailout continues to use the exact-trigger planner path. Ambient pressure is absolute, cylinder pressure is gauge, and gas-use calculations consume gauge-pressure deltas.
